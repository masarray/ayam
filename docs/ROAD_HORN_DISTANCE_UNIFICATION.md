# Road Horn Distance Unification

The road vehicle warning horn now uses one shared front-edge distance for both small and heavy vehicles.

## Why
- Heavy vehicles were honking too far away.
- Small vehicles were honking too close to the chicken.
- A shared midpoint creates a more learnable warning rhythm for players.

## Rule
- Vehicle type still controls horn character: car = Diiiin, heavy vehicle = Thottt.
- Distance timing is shared across all road vehicles.
- Trigger remains based on the vehicle front/nose, not the center/body.
